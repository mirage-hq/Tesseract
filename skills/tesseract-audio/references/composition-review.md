# Composition review

When you compose, have the notes reviewed before you mix: they are cheap to change now and costly once effects, automation and balance are built on them. This is a separate panel before the critic review of the mix, not a replacement for it.

## When to run it

Run it once the notes you write cover the whole piece, before you add accents, effects, automation or the master chain. Parts the user supplied, such as an imported `.mid`, are fixed choices: list them as fixed in the brief. Once you start mixing, the mix review covers any later change to the notes; do not run this panel again.

## Critic panel

Follow the shared critic review, with its pass mark, round limit and reports,
using these roles:

- **Composer**, in the art director's place: whether the hook or motif is worth remembering and develops, melody and phrasing, harmony and voice leading, how the parts share rhythm and register, and contrast and energy across sections, against the brief. In short: the composer decides whether this piece of music represents a good musical progression, according to the RULES OF MUSIC.
- **A music producer in the brief's genre and use**, such as a hip-hop producer for a beat, or a film composer for a bed under picture: whether it would pass for a professional's work in that genre rather than a generic default, whether timing and velocity are written like a player's rather than left mechanical, and whether it does its job, such as leaving room for a voice. In short: the producer's job is to determine whether the vibe fits the brief, and whether it stylistically matches the goal.

Give both critics this scope note: judge the writing only; the sounds, effect and mix are reviewed later.

## Evidence

Give both critics two files: the brief, with tempo, meter, key, length and a section map in bars, and `score.md`, written each round in the critic-input folder from the document. The score lists:

- The tempo and time signatures.
- Each MIDI track with its instrument in a few words, and a drum kit's pads by key and sound, such as 36 kick.
- Each clip once, under its track, with the bars it plays and, for a looping clip, the bars each pass covers.
- Every note a clip plays, a loop's for one pass: its position as bar:beat (5:2.5 is halfway through the second beat of bar 5), its pitch with octave (C4 is middle C, MIDI note 60) or its drum pad, its length in beats, its velocity and any bend.

Give facts, not intent.

## Rounds

Name this panel's folders and checkpoints `composition`, such as `critic-input/composition/` and `Versions/<name>-<request>-composition-round-<n>.json`, so they stay apart from the mix review's. Where the shared review says to deliver, go on to mix that version. Report each round as it describes, and repeat this panel's final scores at delivery.
